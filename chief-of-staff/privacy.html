Privacy Policy

Family Chief of Staff · Last updated: October 4, 2026

Family Chief of Staff is a private set of tools used by a single owner for their own household and businesses. It is not offered to, and has no accounts for, anyone else.

What it accesses from Google

Only with the owner's explicit authorization through Google's OAuth sign-in, and only within the permissions shown on Google's consent screen:

  • Google Calendar, used by the owner's household assistant (Family OS) with the calendar access the owner has authorized.
  • Gmail, send only, used by Family Finance Monitor to send the owner's own financial reports (weekly summaries, exception notices and monthly reports) to the owner and to family members the owner has chosen. It cannot read, search, change or delete any email.

Where the data is stored

Family Finance Monitor keeps the data it uses on the owner's own computer. Its Google access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest, with the encryption key held in the computer's secure keychain, and are never committed to source control or shared.

Sharing and use

Family Finance Monitor's Gmail access is used only to send the financial reports the owner chooses, to the recipients the owner has designated; each recipient receives only the information the owner has allowed them to see. It is not sold, not used for advertising or profiling, and not shared with or disclosed to anyone else.

Family Finance Monitor's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept for as long as the owner uses these tools. The owner can revoke access at any time in their Google Account (Security → Third-party apps with account access), and can delete all stored data by deleting it from their computer.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. Questions can be raised through this site's GitHub repository.
